## ⚡ Engagement & Delivery Protocol

We focus strictly on high-difficulty, enterprise-grade custom data extraction projects.

1. **Target Assessment:** Submit your target URL, domain list, or complex extraction scope.
2. **Proof of Concept (POC):** We extract and deliver a verified **50 to 100-row live sample dataset** directly from your target system to validate schema accuracy and data integrity prior to full execution.
3. **Production Delivery:** Final datasets are delivered fully structured, indexed, and production-ready in your format of choice:
   * **SQLite** (Indexed & query-ready database feeds)
   * **JSON** (Structured / nested payload feeds)
   * **CSV** (Clean, tabular flat-files)
