## ⚡ Engagement & Delivery Protocol

We do **not** handle routine, basic, or entry-level scraping tasks. Our engineering resources are strictly focused on high-difficulty, custom target extraction.

1. **Target Assessment:** Submit your restricted or high-complexity target requirements.
2. **Proof of Concept (POC):** We extract and deliver a verified **50 to 100-row live sample dataset** directly from your target system to validate schema accuracy and data integrity prior to full execution.
3. **Production Delivery:** Final datasets are delivered fully structured and production-ready in your choice of format:
   * **SQLite** (Indexed & query-ready database)
   * **JSON** (Structured / nested object payloads)
   * **CSV** (Clean, tabular flat-files)
